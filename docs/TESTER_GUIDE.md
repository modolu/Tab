# Tab tester guide

1. Open Tab inside Nimiq Pay.
2. Create a small testnet Tab or open a shared Tab.
3. Pick your assigned slot.
4. Connect your Nimiq wallet.
5. Confirm the exact amount and recipient.
6. Approve the payment in Nimiq Pay.
7. Wait for onchain verification and the Paid state.
8. Report any confusing step or error without sending a second payment.
